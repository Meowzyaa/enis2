# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students of Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools (teens, mostly on phones, often
mid-hallway or between classes) checking grades and diary entries. The job:
"what did I get and what's due" in under ten seconds. Parents occasionally
check the report card.

## Product Purpose

NovaNIS is an unofficial, fast, adaptive client for the NIS e-journal. It
exists because the official journal is slow, cluttered and dated. Success is
speed and clarity: log in once, see your diary and report card instantly,
nothing extra.

## Brand Personality

Fast, calm, trustworthy. Quick glanceable data over decoration; soft,
purposeful motion; quiet confidence. Warm coral accent over deep pine keeps
it human without becoming playful.

## Anti-references

- The official NIS journal: cluttered, slow, institutional table-dump UI.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: metric-card grids, gradient-accent everything.
- Gamified school apps (Duolingo-style): no badges, streaks, or confetti.

## Design Principles

1. Ten-second answers: every screen answers its one question at a glance.
2. Data first, chrome second: grades and entries carry the hierarchy, not
   panels around them.
3. Calm motion: entrances and transitions guide the eye, never perform.
4. One system, many skins: theme presets change palette and typeface, never
   layout or behavior.
5. Trust through honesty: loading, error, empty and offline states are
   always distinguishable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: 4.5:1 body-text contrast, full keyboard navigation with
visible focus rings (`default-focus` utility), `prefers-reduced-motion`
honored globally. Mobile-first audience, so touch targets and small
viewports matter.
